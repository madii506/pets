07 · faq

Good questions.

Where does the money go?

100% of creator fees go to Best Friends Animal Society through donate.gg. donate.gg keeps a processing fee.

How do I check?

donate.gg's public board and Solscan. The meter reads donate.gg directly.

Can the charity change?

It is set at launch on pump.fun. donate.gg shows it for every coin.

Is Pets affiliated with Best Friends?

No. Pets is independent. A donation is not an endorsement.

Is $PETS an investment?

No. It is a memecoin. Nothing here is financial advice.
