07 · faq

Good questions.

Short answers, all of them true today.

What is Pets?

A memecoin on Solana, launched as a pump.fun Charity Coin. Every creator fee it earns goes to Best Friends Animal Society through donate.gg, and the meter on this page shows the total as it lands.

Where does the money go, exactly?

Every trade on pump.fun pays a small creator fee. On a Charity Coin, pump.fun sends that fee to donate.gg instead of the creator. donate.gg converts it and pays the charity, keeping a processing fee for doing that.

How do I check it myself?

Every charity coin has its total on donate.gg's public Charity Coins board, and every transfer is on-chain on Solscan. The meter here reads donate.gg directly and never shows a number it did not get from there.

Why Best Friends Animal Society?

They lead the U.S. no-kill movement, work with a network of 6,000 shelter and rescue partners, and publish the national shelter data this page quotes.

Can the charity be changed later?

The charity is set when the coin launches on pump.fun. donate.gg shows the active setting for every coin, so anyone can check it.

Is Pets affiliated with Best Friends, pump.fun or donate.gg?

No. Pets is independent. Best Friends is the listed recipient on donate.gg. A donation is not an endorsement, and none of them run or promote this coin.

Is $PETS an investment?

No. It is a memecoin, and memecoins can go to zero. The only promise is where the creator fees go. Nothing here is financial advice.
